About Tax Services Jersey City

A Jersey City tax practice built around answering the phone and standing behind the number

We started this practice because too many good, honest filers were getting rushed returns, vague pricing, and radio silence between February and April. Here's who we are and how we work.

Why We Exist

Tax preparation shouldn't feel like a black box

Most complaints people have about their accountant aren't about the math. They're about not knowing what something will cost until the bill arrives, not being able to reach anyone between April and the following January, and getting a return back with no explanation of what changed from last year.

Tax Services Jersey City was built to fix that experience for individuals and small businesses in Jersey City and across New Jersey. Every engagement starts with a fixed quote, every return goes through a second reviewer before it's filed, and every client keeps the same point of contact from the first call through the following year's planning conversation.

We work with W-2 employees, retirees, freelancers, LLC owners, and small business operators who want a tax accountant they can actually reach, not just a name on a return.

What Guides The Work

The principles behind every engagement

These aren't slogans on a wall. They're the specific practices that shape how your return gets handled from intake to filing.

Transparency before commitment

You see a fixed price based on your actual documents before any work begins, not an estimate that grows once you're locked in.

Real response times

Calls and emails are answered by a person within one business day, including during the final weeks before the April deadline.

Accuracy over speed

A second preparer reviews every return against your source documents before it's filed with the IRS or the State of New Jersey.

Continuity, not one-off filings

The same preparer works with you year after year, so planning conversations build on real context instead of starting from zero.

How We Work With Clients

What to expect once you reach out

The same structure applies whether you're filing a single W-2 or managing a multi-entity business.

A real conversation first

Before any paperwork, we talk through your situation so the right service and a fixed quote come from an actual understanding of your return, not a generic package.

One preparer, start to finish

The person who scopes your return is the person who prepares it, so you're never repeating your situation to someone new mid-engagement.

A second set of eyes

Every return is checked by a second reviewer against your source documents before anything is submitted to the IRS or New Jersey Division of Taxation.

A relationship that continues

After filing, we stay in touch for planning conversations, notices, and next year's return, instead of disappearing until the following tax season.

Questions About Our Firm

Frequently asked questions

Who will actually prepare my return?+
A dedicated preparer is assigned to your return from the first call through filing, so you're never re-explaining your situation to a new person partway through the engagement.
Are your preparers licensed?+
Yes. Our preparers are licensed to prepare federal and New Jersey state returns and hold active IRS Preparer Tax Identification Numbers (PTINs).
Do you only work with clients during tax season?+
No. We operate year-round for tax planning, IRS and New Jersey Division of Taxation notices, estimated payments, and entity structure questions, not just the filing window.
Can I meet with someone in person?+
Yes, by appointment at our Jersey City office. Phone and video consultations are available for clients anywhere in New Jersey.
How do you handle document security?+
Documents are collected and stored through a secure upload portal rather than email attachments, and access is limited to the preparer and reviewer assigned to your file.
