What's Included

Preparation that reflects how your business is actually structured

A single-member LLC, an S-Corp with payroll, and a multi-entity holding structure all file differently, and mixing them up costs you accuracy and, often, money. We start by confirming your entity type and how it's taxed, then build your federal and New Jersey business returns around that.

Business income doesn't stop at the entity level. Pass-through income from an LLC, S-Corp, or partnership flows to your personal return, so we prepare business and personal returns together to keep the numbers consistent on both sides.

Situations we handle regularly

  • Single-member LLCs reporting on Schedule C or as a disregarded entity
  • Multi-member LLCs and partnerships filing Form 1065
  • S-Corporations, including reasonable compensation and Form 1120-S
  • C-Corporations filing Form 1120
  • Owners with multiple entities or a holding company structure
  • First-year businesses filing an initial return

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you prepare returns for S-Corps and C-Corps, or just LLCs?+
We prepare returns for single-member LLCs, multi-member LLCs and partnerships, S-Corporations, and C-Corporations. Each entity type has different filing requirements, and your quote reflects which one applies to you.
Can you handle multi-entity or holding company structures?+
Yes. We regularly prepare returns for owners with multiple LLCs, a holding company structure, or related entities that need to be filed consistently with each other.
Do you coordinate my business return with my personal return?+
Yes. Pass-through income from your LLC, S-Corp, or partnership flows to your personal return, so we prepare both together to make sure the figures match and nothing is missed.
What if my business hasn't filed in a previous year?+
We can prepare and file prior-year business returns to bring you current, and advise on next steps if the IRS or New Jersey Division of Taxation has sent a notice about a missing filing.
How much does business tax preparation cost?+
Pricing depends on entity type, number of entities, and the complexity of your bookkeeping records. You'll receive a fixed quote after a short review of your financials, before any work begins.
