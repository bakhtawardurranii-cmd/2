Before You Reach Out

Frequently asked questions

How quickly will I hear back after I submit the contact form?+
Within one business day. During the final weeks before the April deadline, response times may run slightly longer, but every message is still answered by a person.
What happens after I submit my information?+
We review what you've shared and schedule a short discovery call to confirm the right service and provide a fixed quote before any work begins.
Do I need to have my documents ready before I contact you?+
No. The first conversation is about understanding your situation. We'll send a document checklist tailored to your return afterward.
Can I schedule a same-week consultation?+
In most cases, yes. Call or submit the form and we'll offer the earliest available phone, video, or in-person slot.
