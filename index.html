Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

What tax services do you offer in Jersey City?+
We offer individual tax preparation, business tax preparation, tax planning and consulting, IRS and New Jersey notice assistance, LLC and S-Corp tax support, and multi-state NY/NJ filing. See our full Tax Services Jersey City guide for details on each.
How much does it cost to hire a tax accountant in Jersey City?+
Cost depends on the complexity of your return. A simple individual return costs less than a small business return with multiple schedules. We provide a fixed quote before any work begins, so there are no surprises.
Do you work with clients outside Jersey City?+
Yes. We serve individuals and businesses throughout Hudson County and New Jersey, and most engagements are handled remotely through secure document upload and video calls.
Can you help if I'm behind on filing previous years?+
Yes. We regularly help individuals and business owners catch up on unfiled prior-year returns and work with the IRS and New Jersey Division of Taxation on notices and payment plans.
What documents do I need to get started?+
For most individual returns you'll need income statements (W-2s or 1099s), records of deductible expenses, and last year's return. Business returns also require bookkeeping records or bank statements. We send a tailored checklist after your first call.
Do you offer tax planning, not just tax filing?+
Yes. Filing reports what already happened. Planning looks ahead, at entity structure, estimated payments, retirement contributions, and timing of income or expenses, so next year's return is smaller and less stressful.
