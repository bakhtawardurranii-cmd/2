What's Included

Preparation that accounts for every income source you actually have

Most individual returns aren't as simple as a single W-2. Between freelance income, an employer in New York while living in New Jersey, a rental property, or a first year of retirement account distributions, the details determine which deductions and credits actually apply.

We review everything you provide and prepare both your federal return and your New Jersey resident return together, so state and federal figures stay consistent.

Situations we handle regularly

  • W-2 employees with straightforward or complex withholding situations
  • New Jersey residents working in New York or another state
  • Freelancers and 1099 contractors filing Schedule C
  • Retirees with pension, Social Security, and IRA or 401(k) distributions
  • Rental property owners filing Schedule E
  • Prior-year and catch-up filings

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you prepare returns for people who work in New York but live in New Jersey?+
Yes. Multi-state returns for New Jersey residents who work in New York or another state are one of the most common situations we handle, including the New Jersey resident credit for taxes paid to another state.
Can you prepare a return for a freelancer or 1099 contractor?+
Yes. We prepare Schedule C returns for freelancers and independent contractors, including self-employment tax calculations and available deductions for business use of a home, equipment, and mileage.
I have income from investments, rental property, or retirement accounts. Can you handle that?+
Yes. Investment income, rental property (Schedule E), and retirement account distributions are all within scope for individual preparation, and each is reflected in your fixed quote based on complexity.
What if I haven't filed in a previous year?+
We can prepare and file prior-year returns to bring you current, and can advise on next steps if you've received a notice from the IRS or New Jersey Division of Taxation about a missing return.
How much does individual tax preparation cost?+
Pricing depends on the number of income sources and schedules required. A straightforward W-2 return costs less than a return with rental income, self-employment, or multi-state filings. You'll receive a fixed quote after a short review of your documents.
