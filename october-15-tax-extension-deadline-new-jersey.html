What the Extension Did and Did Not Do

More time to file is not more time to pay

An extension gives you until October 15 to file your return. It does not extend the time to pay. Tax owed was due in April, and interest and a failure-to-pay penalty generally keep running on any unpaid balance until it is settled. That is why filing as soon as your records are ready usually costs less than waiting for the last day.

New Jersey has its own extension rules, and the conditions are different if tax was owed in April, so we check the state side for you rather than assume it follows the federal extension.

Other things tied to October 15

The FBAR for foreign financial accounts is automatically extended to October 15, so if you reported foreign accounts, this is the same date. See our page on ITIN and foreign income tax help for what that involves.

If the Date Is Already Close or Gone

What to do if you cannot finish by October 15

File as soon as you can. The failure-to-file penalty is generally larger than the failure-to-pay penalty, so a filed return, even with a balance due, usually costs less than an unfiled one. Our guide to filing taxes late in New Jersey explains the penalties and how to catch up.

Business owners whose September 15 extended deadline for an S-corp or partnership return has already passed should act now too, because late-filing penalties for those returns accrue monthly. Our page on business tax preparation in New Jersey covers how we handle them.

Once this return is filed, the next dates to know are in our guide to NJ tax filing deadlines, and the best time to avoid next October's rush is a year-end tax planning conversation.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

When is the extended tax deadline for 2025 returns?+
Thursday, October 15, 2026, for individual returns where an extension was filed on time.
Does an extension give me more time to pay?+
No. An extension extends the time to file, not the time to pay. Interest and a failure-to-pay penalty generally apply to tax not paid by the original April due date.
What happens if I miss October 15?+
File as soon as possible. Penalties grow each month a return is late, so a filed return costs less than an unfiled one even if you cannot pay in full.
Is my New Jersey return automatically extended too?+
New Jersey has its own conditions, particularly if tax was owed in April. We confirm how the state treats your situation when we prepare your return.
Can you realistically finish my return before October 15?+
It depends on how complete your documents are. Send them as soon as you can and we will tell you right away whether the date is realistic.
