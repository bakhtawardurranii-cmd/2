How It's Different From Tax Software

A licensed preparer, delivered remotely, not a form you fill out alone

Online tax filing here doesn't mean a self-service form. It means the same licensed preparer, the same document checklist, and the same two-pass review used for our in-person clients, just conducted by phone, video, and secure upload instead of an office visit.

This service works well if you live outside Jersey City, have a schedule that makes in-person meetings difficult, or simply prefer handling things remotely.

What you can expect

  • A private, secure link to upload your documents, no email attachments
  • A phone or video call to review your situation before preparation begins
  • A walkthrough of your completed return before anything is filed
  • E-filing confirmation once your return is accepted by the IRS and New Jersey

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is online tax filing as accurate as meeting in person?+
Yes. The same preparer, the same two-pass review, and the same document checklist are used whether you file online or in person. The only difference is that meetings happen by phone or video instead of in our office.
How do I send my tax documents securely?+
Through a secure upload portal rather than email attachments. You'll receive a private link after your initial consultation to upload W-2s, 1099s, and other documents.
Do I need to be tech-savvy to file online with you?+
No. The portal is designed to be simple, and if you'd rather send documents by phone photo or scan, we can walk you through it during your consultation call.
Can I still talk to a real person if I file online?+
Yes. Online filing still includes a phone or video consultation with your preparer, not just a form to fill out. You can also call or message with questions at any point.
Do you offer online filing for businesses, or only individuals?+
Both. Online filing is available for individual returns and for business returns, including LLCs, partnerships, and corporations, using the same secure document process.
