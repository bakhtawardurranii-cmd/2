Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Tax Services Jersey City ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers taxservicesjerseycity.com and does not cover information you share with us directly as part of an engagement, such as tax documents, which is governed by our client engagement terms and applicable professional confidentiality standards.

Information we collect

We collect information in a few ways:

  • Information you submit. When you use our contact or consultation request form, we collect what you provide: your name, phone number, email address, and any details you include about your situation.
  • Automatically collected information. Like most websites, we use analytics tools that collect standard technical information, such as your approximate location (city/region level), device and browser type, pages visited, and how you arrived at the site.
  • Communications. If you call, email, or message us, we keep a record of that communication to respond to you and maintain our own business records.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to consultation requests and contact form submissions
  • Communicate with you about services you've asked about
  • Understand how visitors use our website, so we can improve it
  • Meet our own legal and recordkeeping obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services we use

This site relies on a small number of third-party services to operate:

  • Google Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand website traffic and usage patterns. Google may collect information about your visit in accordance with its own privacy policy.
  • Form delivery service. Submissions from our contact and consultation forms are delivered to us through a third-party form-processing service, which transmits your submission to our email inbox.
  • WhatsApp. If you choose to contact us through the WhatsApp button on this site, that conversation is handled through WhatsApp's own platform and is subject to WhatsApp's privacy practices, not this policy.
  • Google Maps. Our contact page embeds a Google Maps view of our office location, which is provided directly by Google.

Each of these services may use cookies or similar technologies as part of how they operate. We don't control, and this policy doesn't cover, how those third parties independently use information they collect.

Cookies

This site uses cookies primarily through Google Analytics, to distinguish visitors and understand site usage. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect how some parts of the site function, though the core content will still be accessible.

How long we keep information

We retain contact form submissions and related communications for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain accurate business records, or as required by applicable law.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you from a contact or consultation form submission, correct it, or delete it, by reaching out through the contact page. We'll respond as promptly as we're able to.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for adults seeking tax and accounting services and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The date below reflects the most recent update.

Contact us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how your information is handled, reach out through our contact page or call 929-746-4698.

Questions about your information?

Reach out and we'll get back to you within one business day.
