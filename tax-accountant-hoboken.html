Local Context

A Manhattan paycheck and a Hoboken address: the two-state return

With PATH, NJ Transit and ferry service at Hoboken Terminal, many residents earn New York wages while living in New Jersey. That usually means a New Jersey resident return and a New York nonresident return, with a credit so the same income is not taxed twice. We prepare them together.

Hoboken has a large share of renters. New Jersey lets renters treat part of their rent as property tax paid on the NJ-1040, which can affect whether the property tax deduction or credit works out better for you. We run both and file the better one. Remote work days can change how New York treats your wages, so we confirm your day counts first.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do Hoboken residents need to file a New York return?+
If you earn wages from a New York employer, usually yes, as a nonresident. You also file a New Jersey resident return and claim a credit for tax paid to New York. We prepare both.
I rent in Hoboken. Does that help my New Jersey return?+
It can. New Jersey allows part of your rent to count as property tax paid, which feeds the deduction or credit on the NJ-1040. We compare both and file the better result.
Do I have to come to Jersey City?+
No. Hoboken is next door, so you can meet at 911 Bergen Ave, or upload documents and finish by phone or video.
