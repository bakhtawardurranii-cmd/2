What "Tax Accountant" Covers

One firm, several ways to work together

"Tax accountant" means different things depending on who's asking. A W-2 employee filing a straightforward return needs something different from an LLC owner managing quarterly estimated payments, and both need something different from a business owner who wants a standing relationship rather than a once-a-year transaction.

This page is the starting point. Below, you'll find the six specific services we offer, so you can go directly to the one that matches your situation instead of reading through content meant for a different kind of client.

What stays the same across every service

Regardless of which service fits you, every engagement includes a fixed quote before work begins, a dedicated preparer who stays with your file from intake through filing, a second-pass review before anything is submitted, and continued availability after your return is filed, for notices, planning, or next year's return.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

What does a tax accountant do that tax software doesn't?+
Software applies the same logic to every return. A tax accountant reviews your actual situation, catches deductions and filing status decisions that software won't flag on its own, and stays available if the IRS or New Jersey Division of Taxation sends a notice afterward.
Which New Jersey tax accountant service is right for me?+
It depends on your situation. Individual filers with W-2 or 1099 income typically need individual tax preparation, business owners need business tax preparation or a small business accountant, and anyone thinking ahead about entity structure or estimated payments benefits from tax planning and consulting.
Do you work with clients anywhere in New Jersey, or only Jersey City?+
We work with clients throughout New Jersey. In-person meetings are available by appointment at our Jersey City office, and remote engagements are available statewide through secure document upload and video calls.
How is pricing determined for tax accountant services?+
Pricing is based on the complexity of your specific return, such as number of income sources, schedules required, and entity type. You receive a fixed quote after a short discovery call, before any work begins.
