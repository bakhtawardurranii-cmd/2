The Short Answer

General price ranges, and why your quote is still fixed

A single W-2 filer with no extras and a Jersey City commuter with a bonus, RSUs, two states and a rental are not the same job, so we give every client a fixed price in writing, based on their actual documents, before any work begins. It does not grow because you had a question or because a form turned up later.

To help you budget, these are typical ranges for work in the Jersey City area. Your quote can land inside or outside them depending on the factors further down this page.

  • Simple individual return (W-2 income, federal and New Jersey): about $150 to $350
  • Commuter return (New York and New Jersey, several W-2s or investments): about $300 to $700
  • Self-employed or 1099 return (Schedule C): about $400 to $900
  • Rental property, equity compensation or foreign account reporting: about $500 to $1,200
  • Business return (S-corp, partnership or multi-member LLC): about $800 to $2,500
  • Monthly bookkeeping: about $250 to $900 a month, depending on transaction volume
  • Response to an IRS or NJ notice: about $200 to $750. Audits and collection cases are scoped individually.

These ranges are a guide, not a quote. If you are comparing preparers, use the factors below to make sure you are comparing the same scope.

What Moves Your Quote

The factors that make one return more work than another

01

Number of income sources

One W-2 is simple. Several W-2s, 1099s, equity compensation and investment statements take longer to reconcile.

02

States involved

A New Jersey-only return is quicker than a commuter filing. See our New York nonresident return preparation.

03

Self-employment income

Schedule C work needs expenses reviewed and estimated payments planned. See our accountant for freelancers and 1099 contractors.

04

Rental property and other schedules

Rental income, a vacation rental or foreign accounts each add forms and checks.

05

Business returns

LLC, S-corp and partnership returns depend on entity type, books and number of owners. See business tax preparation in New Jersey.

06

Condition of your records

Organized books are cheaper to file from. Catch-up work is usually scoped separately, often alongside bookkeepers in Jersey City.

Comparing Preparers

How to choose a tax preparer, whatever you pay

  • Ask for the price in writing before you hand over documents.
  • Be cautious about any preparer who bases a fee on the size of your refund.
  • Paid preparers must sign the return and include their PTIN. Do not sign a blank return.
  • Ask who will actually prepare and who will review your return.
  • Ask whether the preparer is available after April for notices and planning questions.
