What Tax Preparation Covers

From documents to a filed return

Tax preparation is the process of turning your income documents into an accurate, filed return: gathering W-2s, 1099s, and expense records, applying the deductions and credits you're eligible for, preparing federal and New Jersey (and New York, where applicable) returns, and e-filing once you've reviewed and approved the final numbers. We send a document checklist tailored to your situation after a short discovery call, so you know exactly what to gather before we start.

Individuals

Individual tax preparation in Jersey City

Most individual returns we prepare in Jersey City fall into a few buckets: W-2 employees with a single employer, NY/NJ commuters filing in both states, freelancers with 1099 income, and retirees drawing pension or investment income. Each requires a different set of forms and deductions, which is why we scope a fixed quote based on your actual documents rather than a flat rate that assumes everyone's return looks the same. For self-employed filers, tax preparation for self-employed individuals also includes reviewing what's deductible against 1099 income and making sure quarterly estimated payments are on track, so April isn't a surprise.

Businesses

Business and LLC tax preparation in Jersey City

Business tax preparation starts with confirming entity structure: a single-member LLC is generally reported on your personal return by default, a multi-member LLC files a partnership return, and an LLC or corporation that has elected S-Corp status files its own return with payroll considerations for the owner. Small-business tax preparation for Jersey City clients typically also involves coordinating with your bookkeeping records, since accurate books make for a faster, cleaner return.

For ongoing business relationships rather than a once-a-year filing, see Small Business Tax Accountant. For entity-structure questions specifically, see LLC vs. S-Corp in New Jersey.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

What's included in tax preparation?+
Reviewing your income documents, applying eligible deductions and credits, preparing federal and New Jersey (and New York, if applicable) returns, and e-filing once you've approved the final version.
Do you prepare returns for self-employed individuals?+
Yes. We regularly prepare Schedule C returns for freelancers and independent contractors, including tracking deductible business expenses and calculating quarterly estimated tax payments.
Can you prepare an LLC's tax return?+
Yes. We prepare returns for single-member LLCs, multi-member LLCs, and LLCs that have elected S-Corp status, along with the payroll and reasonable-compensation considerations that come with an S-election.
How long does tax preparation take?+
A straightforward individual return can often be completed within a few business days of receiving your documents. Business returns and multi-state returns typically take longer, depending on complexity.
