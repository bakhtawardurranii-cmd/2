The Full Picture

Everything under "tax services," explained

"Tax services" covers more ground than a single tax return. For Jersey City residents and business owners, it typically means some combination of tax preparation (getting a return filed correctly and on time), tax planning (making decisions during the year that reduce next year's tax bill), tax consulting (getting advice on a specific question, like entity structure or a large one-time transaction), and, when something goes wrong, help responding to an IRS or New Jersey Division of Taxation notice.

We're based at 911 Bergen Ave in Jersey City and work with clients across Downtown, Journal Square, Grove Street, the waterfront, and Bergen-Lafayette, both in person and remotely. Below is a breakdown of each service area we cover, so you can see exactly what applies to your situation before booking a consultation.

01 · For Individuals

Individual tax preparation and filing

Most Jersey City individual returns we prepare fall into one of a few categories: W-2 employees with a straightforward return, NY/NJ commuters who need a multi-state return, freelancers and 1099 contractors reporting self-employment income, and retirees with pension or investment income. We prepare and e-file federal and New Jersey state returns, and New York returns for commuters, with a fixed quote before we start.

For a deeper look at how we handle individual returns, including deductions, quarterly estimated taxes, and what documents you'll need, see Individual Tax Preparation and Tax Preparation Jersey City.

02 · For Businesses

Business tax preparation: LLCs, S-Corps, and small businesses

Business tax services cover LLC tax preparation, S-Corp returns, partnership returns, and multi-entity structures. A single-member LLC is generally taxed as a sole proprietorship by default, reported on your personal return, while a multi-member LLC or a business that has elected S-Corp status files its own return. Which structure applies changes your deadlines, your paperwork, and in some cases your total tax bill, which is why entity structure is usually the first thing we confirm with a new business client.

For small businesses that want an ongoing relationship rather than a once-a-year filing, see Small Business Tax Accountant, which covers quarterly check-ins and coordinated bookkeeping. For the full business return process, see Business Tax Preparation.

LLC and S-Corp tax considerations

LLC owners often ask whether electing S-Corp status makes sense once profits reach a certain level, since it can reduce self-employment tax but adds payroll and "reasonable compensation" requirements. We walk through this trade-off as part of tax planning, not as a one-time decision made in isolation. See LLC vs. S-Corp in New Jersey and S-Corp Reasonable Compensation for the specifics.

03 · Forward-Looking

Tax planning and tax consulting

Filing reports what already happened. Planning and consulting look ahead, at entity structure, quarterly estimated payments, retirement contributions, and the timing of income or major expenses, so next year's return is smaller and less stressful. This is usually most valuable for business owners, freelancers with variable income, and anyone going through a change like a new business, a home sale, or a new dependent.

See Tax Planning & Consulting for how engagements typically work, and Quarterly Estimated Taxes for NJ Freelancers if you're self-employed and want to avoid an underpayment penalty.

04 · When Something Goes Wrong

IRS and New Jersey tax notice assistance

An IRS or New Jersey Division of Taxation letter isn't automatically bad news, but it does need a response, and the right response depends entirely on what the notice actually says. We review the notice, explain it in plain language, and handle next steps, whether that's a simple correction, a documentation request, or negotiating a payment plan on a balance due.

We also help clients catch up on unfiled prior-year returns, which is one of the more common reasons people reach out to us outside of tax season. See What to Do If You Receive an IRS or NJ Tax Notice and Filing Taxes Late in New Jersey for more detail.

05 · Jersey City-Specific

NY/NJ multi-state tax situations

Jersey City's position across the Hudson River means a significant share of residents work in Manhattan, which creates a multi-state filing situation: a nonresident New York return for income earned there, plus a New Jersey resident return that claims a credit for taxes paid to New York. Getting the credit calculation right is the difference between a clean multi-state return and an unnecessary double tax. This is one of the most common return types we prepare for Jersey City clients specifically. See Filing Taxes in Both NY and NJ for a full walkthrough.

06 · Supporting Your Books

Bookkeeping and accounting support

We're not a full-service bookkeeping firm, but bookkeeping quality directly affects how clean and accurate a business return is, so we work with your existing bookkeeping records year-round, flag issues before they become filing problems, and can refer you to a bookkeeper we trust if you need books built from scratch. For small business clients on an ongoing plan, this coordination happens as part of quarterly check-ins. See 5 Bookkeeping Habits for Tax Season for practical habits that make filing easier.

Frequently asked questions

What tax services are available in Jersey City?+
Individual tax preparation, business tax preparation for LLCs and S-Corps, tax planning and consulting, IRS and New Jersey Division of Taxation notice help, multi-state NY/NJ filing, and bookkeeping coordination for small businesses.
Do you handle both individual and business tax returns?+
Yes. Most of our Jersey City clients are individuals with W-2 or 1099 income, but we also prepare business returns for LLCs, S-Corps, and partnerships, and many clients use us for both their personal and business filings.
Can you help with an IRS or New Jersey tax notice?+
Yes. We review the notice, explain what it means in plain language, and handle the response, whether that's a correction, documentation request, or setting up a payment plan.
Do you work with LLCs and S-Corps in Jersey City?+
